About me

I am a PhD researcher at KU Leuven and, since November 2026, a PhD Fellow in Strategic Basic Research of the Research Foundation Flanders (FWO). I work in the lab of Prof. Alexander Bertrand, under his supervision and that of Dr. Simon Geirnaert and Prof. Tom Francart (ExpORL). My research concerns EEG-based auditory attention decoding: identifying from neural recordings which speaker a listener attends to in a multi-talker environment. I focus on algorithms that remain reliable under real-world variability, without subject-specific calibration, as a step towards neuro-steered hearing devices.

I hold a master's degree in Biomedical Engineering from Politecnico di Torino, which included an Erasmus semester at Trinity College Dublin. My thesis applied transformer models to vocal signals in Parkinson's disease. Before joining KU Leuven, I held a postgraduate research fellowship at Politecnico di Torino, where I developed and validated algorithms for sleep disorder assessment.
