Outside the lab

Outreach and mentoring

Since 2023 I have worked with initiatives that bring more women into engineering. With WeAreHERe (PoliWO) at Politecnico di Torino I mentored prospective and first-year students and helped organise awareness events about what studying engineering actually involves.

With the IEEE-HKN Mu Nu Chapter I have been part of the Corporate Training area since March 2024, organising workshops and seminars that connect students with people working in industry and research.
