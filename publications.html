01 2026 Biomedical Signal Processing
and Control · 113(A), 108954

Voice classification in Parkinson's disease: a deep learning approach using transformers and error rate metrics

Perrone, B. · Amato, F. · Olmo, G.

Accuracy alone does not tell you whether a model has learned pathology or learned the microphone. We paired a transformer pipeline with error-rate metrics that make its decisions inspectable.

02 2026 Algorithms
19(7), 531

Full-night comparison of ECG- and PPG-derived measures of cardiac variability for sleep disorder screening

Ciampa, I. · Perrone, B. · et al.

Wearables promise sleep screening from the wrist. We ran the comparison across entire nights rather than short windows, and identified where pulse rate variability stops being a safe substitute for heart rate variability.
