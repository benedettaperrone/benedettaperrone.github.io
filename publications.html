and Control · 113(A), 108954

Voice classification in Parkinson's disease: a deep learning approach using transformers and error rate metrics

Perrone, B. · Amato, F. · Olmo, G.

Speech changes affect most people with Parkinson's disease, and often appear early. We asked whether the mistakes an automatic transcription system makes on a recording can stand in for a clinical severity score, and paired that measure with transformer models trained across three clinical datasets and two languages.

19(7), 531

Full-night comparison of ECG- and PPG-derived measures of cardiac variability for sleep disorder screening

Ciampa, I. · Perrone, B. · et al.

A wrist wearable measures the pulse, not the electrical activity of the heart, and the two are widely assumed to be interchangeable. We compared them over entire nights in fifty people, healthy and with sleep disorders, and worked out which variability measures survive that comparison and which quietly do not.

A review on computational tools for analytical visualisation and molecular interactions of sncRNAs: prospects in NDDs

Panagiotopoulos, K. · Ibanibo, L. · Perrone, B. · et al.

Written during an undergraduate research placement at Politecnico di Torino. The review gathers the computational tools and databases available for predicting small non-coding RNA interactions, and for the network analysis and visualisation that follows, in the context of neurodevelopmental impairment in preterm children.

Theses

04 2024 M.Sc. thesis
Politecnico di Torino

Voice classification in Parkinson's disease using transformer models and error rate metrics

Perrone, B. · supervisors: Olmo, G. · Amato, F.

The study behind the Biomedical Signal Processing and Control article. Vision and audio spectrogram transformers were trained on vocal recordings from three clinical datasets, and word and character error rates were used to relate what the models pick up to clinical severity scores.
